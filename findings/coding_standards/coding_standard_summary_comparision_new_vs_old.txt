SIMPLE ANALOGY
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

Before:
--------
You had a calculator with the numbers 5 and 10 permanently soldered onto
the circuit board. Any change required modifying the hardware itself.

Now:
----
You have a configurable calculator where the values are entered externally.
The calculation engine remains unchanged while the inputs can be adjusted
whenever needed.

CONCLUSION
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

The DAL-aware, configuration-driven architecture represents a professional,
industry-aligned approach to software quality assurance. It improves
flexibility, auditability, maintainability, scalability, and compliance
with DO-178C principles. By separating validation rules from validation
logic, the tool becomes easier to certify, easier to maintain, and more
suitable for safety-critical software development environments.
